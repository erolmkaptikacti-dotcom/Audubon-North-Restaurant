Our Story

A neighborhood spot with no shortcuts.

Tucked off SweetHome Road in Amherst, Audubon North has been quietly feeding Western New York's most loyal regulars for years. What started as a neighborhood spot became a pilgrimage destination — all because of one man's obsession with doing everything from scratch.

Scott, the owner, doesn't cut corners. Every chicken finger is hand-split from whole breast, hand-breaded with homemade crumbs, and finished on the grill. The kimmelweck rolls come out of their own oven. The bleu cheese is made in-house. Even the bread crumbs.

This is Buffalo food done right. Not fast. Not factory. Real.

What We Make

Everything is handmade.

A focused menu done with uncommon care. Nothing frozen. Nothing corporate.

BBQ Chicken Fingers

Whole chicken breasts, hand-split, hand-breaded with homemade crumbs, deep fried then finished on the grill. These are not your typical fingers — they're enormous, extraordinary, and made by someone who cares.

House Signature
Grilled Wings

Double-dipped and grilled to order. Consistently called top-5 best wings in Buffalo. Served with our house-made bleu cheese — some of the best in the city.

Buffalo Legend
Beef on Weck

Piled on a kimmelweck roll baked in-house — one of the best in Western New York. Served with horseradish, pickle slices, and carrots. As Western New York as it gets.

WNY Classic
Friday Fish Fry

A Western New York Friday ritual done properly. Tender fish, perfectly fried, with sides that surprise — think stuffed onions, house slaw, and pasta.

Fridays Only
House Bleu Cheese

Made from scratch in-house. Customers drive here just for this. The perfect companion to wings, fingers, and anything else on the menu.

Salads & Sides

Fresh-cut fries, big salads, garlic pasta, and rotating sides that always manage to surprise. Great value, generous portions — nothing is an afterthought here.
